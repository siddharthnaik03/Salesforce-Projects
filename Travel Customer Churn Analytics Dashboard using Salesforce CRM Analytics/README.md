# Travel Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard 

### Project Description

Developed an interactive Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard in Salesforce CRM Analytics to analyze customer attrition patterns in a travel and hospitality business. The solution uses CRM Analytics Datasets, Recipes, Transformations, KPIs, and Visualizations to identify key factors influencing customer churn and provide actionable business insights.

The dashboard tracks overall customer churn, active customers, churn rate percentage, and analyzes churn behavior across age groups, income segments, hotel booking status, frequent flyer status, and social media connectivity. Custom recipes and calculated fields were used to derive churn metrics and generate meaningful business insights for decision-making.

### Requirement

* Identify customers who are likely to churn.
* Measure overall churn rate.
* Analyze churn trends across different customer segments.
* Understand factors contributing to customer attrition.

### Key Findings
* Overall churn rate is 23.48%.
* Customers aged 25–30 contribute the highest churn volume.
* Low-income customers show higher churn behavior compared to other income segments.
* Frequent flyer customers contribute significantly to churn.
* Customers with hotel booking history show higher churn compared to non-bookers.
* Social media connectivity influences customer retention patterns.
